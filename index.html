About

From botanical garden to vertical frame.

I'm 40, and I recently moved to Florida and I'm starting a lot of things fresh here, including on camera. Before that, I spent years between science and finance : reading data, tracking numbers, and making sense of things that looked messy on paper.

In UGC, what keeps me here isn't the camera, it's people. I love thinking about the brand, the product, and the person on the other side of the screen who's deciding whether to buy it. I'm genuinely curious about what works and why.
